Hero Goggles

Find an influencer at KubeCon by their UWB beacon: distance and direction on an iPhone, in the Meta Ray-Ban Display lens, and aloud, with a live map of the venue.

What it is

Hero Goggles finds a person in a crowd. At KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026, the people we looked for were two influencers, Kelsey Hightower and Kunal Kushwaha, each carrying a small Ultra Wideband (UWB) beacon. An iPhone ranges the beacon and gives the distance and the way to it on the phone, in the lens of Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses, and aloud. Every reading also reaches a live map of the Salt Palace that anyone can open.

In the lens

A web app for Meta Ray-Ban Display: a list, a map and Find, driven by the Neural Band.

On the iPhone

hero-goggles-ios ranges the beacon over UWB and draws the seek in the lens through Meta's toolkit.

On the live map

hero-goggles.darkshift.cloud/live/: the venue, the beacons, the registry, influencer pages and Find by voice.

For agents

influence-api's MCP tools and WebMCP in the browser: ask an agent to "find Kunal".

How a find works

  1. Someone says who to find: on the glasses, by voice on the map, or through an agent's find_influencer tool. influence-api sets that seeker's target.
  2. The iPhone ranges the target's beacon (a Qorvo DWM3001CDK running Qorvo's Nearby Interaction firmware) and posts each reading to influence-api.
  3. influence-api works out distance alerts (50, 30, 20, 10 and 5 ft, found at 3 ft, lost) and streams the state to the map.
  4. The lens and the map show the distance and the alerts; Skyhook Radar confirms each alert from the cluster's event timeline.
